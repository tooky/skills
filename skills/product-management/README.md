# Product Management

Skills for shaping product bets. Ours alone (not part of Matt's set), so no `docs/` page; they ship in the plugin.

- **[grill-bet](./grill-bet/SKILL.md)**: Get grilled about a product bet until the problem is sharp, and leave with a PRD-lite or PR/FAQ. User-invoked: type it.
- **[bet-writing](./bet-writing/SKILL.md)**: The problem-first writing discipline behind grill-bet: the nodes a bet must answer, the two formats, and the market-facing voice of a press release. Model-invoked.
