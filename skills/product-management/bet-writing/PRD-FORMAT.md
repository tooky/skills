# PRD-lite format

Create when the format is chosen. Update sections as nodes settle. Readable in ten minutes; past two pages, something belongs in an appendix.

```markdown
# <Bet title>
*<One line: what this is, who it's for, any placeholder terms.>*

## The problem
1. What customers are trying to do and why it matters.
2. Why the world doesn't line up, with a concrete example in their domain.
3. What they do today to bridge it and why that fails at scale. Name the workaround.
4. Why now: what changed, and the cost of standing still.

## Who this is for
Two to four audiences, each with the decision or action they take. Say which are existing accounts and which are new to us, and what is different for someone who doesn't use us today.

## What has to be true
Two to four scenarios, one short paragraph each: a real team, a real thing they own in domain language, what they can do that they can't today. At least one boundary case. Describe outcomes, never screens.

## Success
What each customer would say made it worth it; what internal signal moves.

## Non-goals
What we're deliberately not doing, and why.

## Open questions
One flat list. Tag each item `[engineering]` (theirs to decide), `[customer]` (only they can answer), or `[unknown]` (nobody knows yet).
```
